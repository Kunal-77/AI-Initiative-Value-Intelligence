# Purpose

This document compares the Value Intelligence platform with adjacent
products to identify opportunities for differentiation.

## Market Categories

### Business Execution

-   Jira
-   Asana
-   Monday.com
-   Linear

Strength: Project execution.

Gap: Do not measure business value created by initiatives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### BI & Analytics

-   Power BI
-   Tableau
-   Looker

Strength: Excellent dashboards.

Gap: Require customers to build reports and do not manage initiative
lifecycle or value governance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Cloud Cost Management

-   AWS Cost Explorer
-   Azure Cost Management
-   CloudZero
-   Finout
-   Vantage

Strength: Cost visibility and allocation.

Gap: Stop at spend analysis. They do not connect spend to business
outcomes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### SaaS Spend Management

-   Zylo
-   Torii
-   Spendflo
-   Productiv

Strength: SaaS discovery, renewals, licenses.

Gap: Limited initiative-level business value measurement.

### IT Finance & Technology Business Management (TBM)

-   IBM Apptio
-   Planview (VSM/PPM)
-   ServiceNow APM/ITBM

Strength: Comprehensive cost allocation, resource mapping, portfolio management, and value-stream analysis.

Gap: Extremely heavy configuration, long onboarding cycles, and static reports. They lack a lightweight, evidence-aware lifecycle and context-aware AI decision explanations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Personal Finance

-   Rocket Money
-   Monarch Money
-   Copilot Money
-   YNAB

Strength: Budgeting and subscription tracking.

Gap: Do not evaluate whether subscriptions create sufficient personal
value.

# Value Intelligence Position

Value Intelligence focuses on one question:

> Is this investment creating enough value?

Business: - Initiative governance - KPI tracking - Financial tracking -
Evidence collection - AI-supported decision making

Personal (future): - Subscription intelligence - AI tool spending -
Renewal optimization - Personal value scoring

# Differentiators

1.  Evidence-backed decisions.
2.  Initiative lifecycle governance.
3.  Deterministic KPI and ROI calculations.
4.  AI explanations instead of dashboards alone.
5.  Shared platform with independent Business and Personal workspaces.

# Risks

-   Long sales cycles in enterprise.
-   Integration complexity.
-   Trust depends on data quality.
-   Need to validate willingness to pay.

# Opportunities

-   AI ROI management.
-   FinOps + business value.
-   Executive decision intelligence.
-   Personal subscription optimization.
-   Unified Value Intelligence ecosystem.
