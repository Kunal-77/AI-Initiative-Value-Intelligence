# Executive Summary

The Personal Platform is an independent workspace within the Value
Intelligence ecosystem.

Its purpose is to help individuals understand whether their recurring
expenses, subscriptions, memberships, software, AI tools, and digital
services create enough value for the money they spend.

Unlike traditional budgeting apps, the platform focuses on value rather
than expense tracking alone.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

Help individuals make smarter financial decisions by measuring the value
created by every recurring investment.

# Core Problem

Most users know:

-   What they spend.

Very few know:

-   Which subscriptions they actually use.
-   Which services overlap.
-   Which renewals should be cancelled.
-   How much they spend on AI tools.
-   How much recurring software costs annually.

# Relationship to Business Platform

The Personal Platform is a separate workspace.

It does not share business initiatives, business KPIs, financial records
or organization data.

Only authentication, user profile, notification framework, design system, AI infrastructure, audit logging, and settings framework may be shared across the ecosystem.
