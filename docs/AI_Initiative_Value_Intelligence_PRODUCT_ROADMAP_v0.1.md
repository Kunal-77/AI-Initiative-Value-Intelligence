# AI Initiative Value Intelligence

## Product Roadmap

**Version:** v0.1\
**Status:** Draft\
**Owner:** Product Management\
**Last Updated:** August 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the Value Intelligence
platform for both Business and Personal products.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 1 --- Foundation (Current)

## Business

-   Initiative management
-   Business case
-   Financial tracking
-   Measurement plans
-   Baselines
-   KPI assignments
-   Lifecycle management

## Personal

-   Not started

Goal: Deliver a stable MVP for measuring initiative value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 2 --- Enterprise Integrations

Business: - AWS - Azure - Google Cloud - Jira - GitHub - Slack -
Microsoft Teams - Salesforce - HubSpot

Capabilities: - Automatic evidence collection - Live KPI
synchronization - Cloud cost ingestion

Goal: Reduce manual data entry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 3 --- AI Decision Intelligence

Business: - AI initiative summaries - ROI explanations - KPI
recommendations - Baseline suggestions - Risk detection - Initiative
health scoring

Goal: Move from reporting to intelligent recommendations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 4 --- Personal Platform

Features: - Subscription management - OTT tracking - AI subscriptions -
Domain renewals - Bills - Insurance - Credit cards - Investments -
Spending dashboard

Goal: Become the personal operating system for recurring expenses.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 5 --- Predictive Intelligence

Business: - ROI forecasting - Budget prediction - Initiative failure
prediction - Capacity planning

Personal: - Spending forecasts - Cash-flow prediction - Smart budgeting - AI subscription advisor - Renewal recommendations - Spending insights

Goal: Predict future outcomes before they happen.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Phase 6 --- Autonomous Intelligence

Business: - Autonomous executive reports - AI portfolio optimization -
Automated recommendations

Personal: - AI financial assistant - Automatic renewal decisions -
Subscription optimization

Goal: Create an AI assistant that continuously improves financial and
strategic decisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Success Criteria

-   Less manual reporting
-   Higher ROI visibility
-   Better executive decisions
-   Reduced subscription waste
-   Strong enterprise integrations
-   AI-driven recommendations

------------------------------------------------------------------------
